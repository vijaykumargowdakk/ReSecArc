Security findings

Design-time checks based on IEC 62443 zone and conduit principles, plus company policy rules. Not a compliance assessment.

SeverityFindingDetailReferenceSuggested fix
highIEC 62443 communication violationOffice workstation (L4) connects directly to Pump station RTU (L1)IEC 62443-3-2 ZCR 3.2; IEC 62443-3-3 SR 5.1, SR 5.2Route the connection through the IT/OT firewall and terminate it in the DMZ (L3.5).
highIEC 62443 communication violationOperations switch (zone Operations) and Control switch (zone Control) are linked with no boundary device between the zonesIEC 62443-3-3 SR 5.2Put a firewall on the conduit between the two zones, or move both devices into the same zone.
highIEC 62443 configuration issueDosing safety controller (zone Safety) is linked to Chemical dosing PLC (zone Control) without a boundary deviceIEC 62443-3-2 ZCR 3.3Give the safety system its own zone and put a boundary device between it and other systems.
mediumIEC 62443 communication violationModbus from SCADA server (zone Operations) to Filtration PLC (zone Control) crosses zones without authentication or encryptionIEC 62443-3-3 SR 3.1, SR 4.1Turn on the protocol’s security option (TLS, secure authentication or its secure variant), or keep the flow inside one zone.
