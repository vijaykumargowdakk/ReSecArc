# Getting help

- **How do I…?** Start with the [README](README.md); it is the full guide to every feature, with a [feature guide](README.md#4-feature-guide), [typical workflows](README.md#5-typical-workflows) and [keyboard shortcuts](README.md#26-keyboard-shortcuts).
- **Something is wrong:** open a [bug report](https://github.com/vijaykumargowdakk/ReSecArc/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
- **An idea:** open a [feature request](https://github.com/vijaykumargowdakk/ReSecArc/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml), or talk it through first in [Discussions → Ideas](https://github.com/vijaykumargowdakk/ReSecArc/discussions/categories/ideas).
- **A question that isn't covered:** ask in [Discussions → Q&A](https://github.com/vijaykumargowdakk/ReSecArc/discussions/categories/q-a). Answers there stay findable for the next person.
- **Show what you built:** share a design (fictional or anonymised, never a real plant's details) in [Discussions → Show and tell](https://github.com/vijaykumargowdakk/ReSecArc/discussions/categories/show-and-tell).
- **A security problem:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Never in a public issue.
- **A commercial licence** (to use or modify ReSecArc without the AGPL-3.0's obligations): contact the copyright holder through https://github.com/vijaykumargowdakk. See [NOTICE](NOTICE).
